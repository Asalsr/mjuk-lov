## 2. Why we landed here

> The *why* behind every major decision. Future-you will thank present-you for writing this down.

### 2.1 The name — why Mjuk Lov

**Names evaluated and dropped**: Doppa, Vispa, Pricka, Krollan, Mjuk & Co (too generic when combined with "& Co" — that suffix exists in many other brands), Mjukland (too childish, kept pulling toward kids' brands), Verkstad, Stilla, Råform, Mjukverk, Hantverket.

**Why Mjuk Lov won**:
- Two single syllables → most premium-feeling structure in branding (compare: Cos, Ace, Muji, Aesop, Frame)
- Layered meaning: *mjuk* (the texture, the cake) + *lov* (the promise, the vow)
- *Lov* in Swedish also means "school holiday" — subtle celebration association
- English ear hears "mjuk love" — warmth for non-Swedish customers without trying
- Domains `mjuklov.se` and `mjuklov.com` both free at the time of check
- `@mjuklov` free on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Pinterest

**Final legal verification still required before locking** (you may have done these by now — re-verify if not):
- Bolagsverket company register: foretagsinfo.bolagsverket.se → search "Mjuk Lov" + "Mjuklov"
- PRV trademark register: prv.se → search same names in class 30 (food/baked goods) and class 43 (food services)
- Both are free, take ~20 minutes total

### 2.2 The brand personality

**Customer profile decided**:
- DIY kit buyer: 20–40, Instagram-native, design-conscious, gift buyer
- Corporate buyer: small SME (10–30 people) — creative agencies, tech startups, design studios. **Not** large corporates like Volvo or AstraZeneca.

These two buyers turned out to be roughly the same person in different contexts — a 32-year-old design-aware office manager and a 32-year-old design-aware gift buyer. That collapsed two audiences into one and simplified the brand significantly.

**Voice decided**: Warm + slightly playful, never silly. Self-aware. Slightly clever. Always confident. No exclamation marks. No emojis in marketing copy.

**Language balance decided**: Bilingual Swedish + English equally everywhere. Swedish gives cultural authenticity and local search; English gives reach via Instagram.

**Voice grammar decided**: "I" for personal moments (Instagram captions, behind-the-scenes), "we" for professional (website, B2B materials, packaging copy). The site uses "we" because it's the professional surface.

**Founder visibility decided**: Anonymous at launch, reveal slowly. Around month 4–6 — when audience is invested and curious — do a single "founder reveal" post (hands, cropped portrait, three-paragraph story). It will likely be your highest-performing post of year one.

### 2.3 The product strategy — Path A vs Path B

The project has two strategic shapes in tension. Both are valid. They aren't equally suited to the current moment.

**Path A — Original two-line plan**: Launch corporate subscriptions and DIY cake kits in parallel. Both visible on the website from day one. 8–10 rotating recipes by month two. Year 1 target SEK 280,000–370,000 base.

- Strengths: Maximum revenue potential, broader footprint
- Weaknesses: Operationally complex from day one. One person, one home kitchen, perfecting 8+ recipes while running a brand, doing deliveries, marketing, and acquiring corporate clients.

**Path B — Single-product launch**: Pick one product. Perfect it. Sell only that for 8–16 weeks. Add a second product only when the first is rock-solid.

- Strengths: Quality, focus, sharper marketing, simpler COGS, lower risk
- Weaknesses: Slower revenue ramp, narrower footprint at launch

**Current direction**: Path B, with **tiramisu** as the single product.

**Why tiramisu specifically**:
- Premium per-unit margin (75–95 kr for a single-serve)
- Photogenic by nature (glass jar, dusted cocoa, espresso-stained ladyfinger)
- Restaurant-friendly format — a finished portion in a container, no plating skill required from the chef. Many Italian, wine bar, and brunch venues in Gothenburg don't make their own.
- Easy reorder cycle — customers buy one, love it, order again next week
- Production scales linearly with no technique cliff

**Tiramisu's downsides — real but manageable**:
- Raw-egg dessert → food safety risk. **Mitigation**: use pasteurized egg yolks (Menigo and KåKå carry them) or the *zabaglione* method (yolks cooked to 71°C over a bain-marie before folding into mascarpone). Either eliminates the salmonella risk.
- Shelf life 48–72 hours → tight logistics, no freezer buffer
- No DIY kit version possible (you can't ship an unassembled tiramisu)

## 3. The brand bible

### 3.1 Positioning

Mjuk Lov is a premium home-based dessert brand in Gothenburg that promises softness in everything it makes — in taste, in texture, in the experience of giving and receiving. Hand-baked. Bilingual. Quiet. Confident. Restrained. **Not Swedish-cuisine** — Swedish in name only, international in product.

### 3.2 Voice

- **Tone**: Warm + slightly playful, never silly. Self-aware. Slightly clever. Always confident.
- **Pacing**: Short, punchy sentences. Occasional wry humor about the work itself.
- **No-go list**: No exclamation marks. No emojis in marketing copy. No "amazing," "yummy," "delicious." No "we're so excited to..." No empty hype words.

**Sample captions in voice**:
- *"Cardamom, three ways. The third way might be a bit much. We did it anyway."*
- *"This week's office delivery: almond, lemon curd, a thread of brown butter. The kind of Wednesday people actually look forward to."*
- *"Hand-piped, slightly imperfect, exactly as intended."*
- *"Made three. Photographed two. Ate one. (For research.)"*

**Sample longer copy** (current website):
- *"Mjuk Lov började i ett litet kök i Linnéstaden. Inte som ett företag, utan som ett svar på en fråga: vad händer när du ger bort något du bakat själv?"*
- *"Home baked in Gothenburg. For ordinary Wednesdays and occasions that deserve more."*

### 3.3 Visual rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| One light tone per surface | Mixing cream, peach, and apricot in one composition kills coherence |
| One accent color only | The dusty terracotta is the *one* saturated color doing the emotional work |
| Wordmark stays in cocoa or wine | Never in pink or peach — the dark anchor is what keeps the brand grown-up |
| Photography on a single light surface | Multi-color backgrounds break the editorial feel |
| Negative space is the brand | Don't fill every corner |
| No drop shadows | Anywhere, ever |
| No emojis in marketing copy | Voice carries the warmth |

### 3.5 Packaging philosophy

**Quietly thoughtful unboxing** — not stuffed, but with small unexpected touches (small card, extra candle, sprig of dried lavender for the kit version).

**Hefte concept** — small folded A5 or DL-fold card insert. Not a real bound booklet (too expensive at small volumes). Contents per issue:
- Welcome note in voice
- Season's story
- Illustrated step-by-step (for kits) or product origin (for tiramisu)
- "Did you know" about one ingredient
- Signature
- Invitation to tag @mjuklov

**Same visual system, different format** — kit boxes and corporate trays share: wordmark, palette, wax seal, paper stock, motifs. They differ in shape (square gift box vs long flat tray) and what goes inside.

**Per-unit packaging cost target**: ~SEK 27–39 per cake kit, ~SEK 12–18 per corporate delivery, ~SEK 7–10 per tiramisu jar (jar + lid + label + tissue).

## 4. Product strategy

### 4.1 Phase 1 — Tiramisu, single product (Months 1–2)

- **Product**: Single-serve tiramisu in glass jars (170–200 ml). One recipe, perfected.
- **Channels**: Corporate Friday delivery (8 / 12 / 20-jar boxes for SME offices) + individual pickup orders
- **Volume target**: 80–120 jars/week by end of month 2
- **Spoilage target**: Under 3%
- **No restaurants yet**. **No DIY kit yet**. Website Kits section either hidden or marked "coming soon."

**Food safety setup**:
- Pasteurized egg yolks (Menigo carries them) OR zabaglione method (cook yolks to 71°C over bain-marie before folding into mascarpone)
- Documented HACCP plan including raw-egg controls
- Daily batch dates on every jar
- Refrigeration log

### 4.2 Phase 2 — Restaurant wholesale (Months 3–4)

- **Channel**: 2–3 small Italian, Mediterranean, or wine-bar venues in Gothenburg
- **Pricing**: ~50% of direct retail (restaurants resell at 2× minimum)
- **Logistics**: Daily or three-times-weekly delivery
- **Minimum order**: 24 jars per order (protects margin)
- **Why now**: You have production rhythm. Spoilage rate is known. You can absorb a cancelled order without losing the week.

### 4.3 Phase 3 — Second product (Months 5–8)

Decision deferred. Don't pre-commit. Options:

- **Adjacent Italian**: panna cotta, affogato kit, cannoli — keeps the supply chain and brand consistent
- **DIY kit / cake direction**: layer cake or seasonal kit — opens the original two-line plan
- **Seasonal-only**: saffron-spiced something for Lucia, semlor for the Feb–Mar window

The choice should be informed by what you learn from Phase 1+2.

### 5.8 Wiring real email submissions

Currently the form saves to localStorage. To send real emails:

```bash
pnpm add resend
```

Create `app/api/order/route.ts`:

```ts
import { Resend } from 'resend';
const resend = new Resend(process.env.RESEND_API_KEY);

export async function POST(req: Request) {
  const body = await req.json();
  await resend.emails.send({
    from: 'Mjuk Lov <orders@mjuklov.se>',
    to: 'hej@mjuklov.se',
    subject: `New ${body.type} inquiry from ${body.name}`,
    text: JSON.stringify(body, null, 2)
  });
  return Response.json({ ok: true });
}
```

In `Order.tsx`, replace localStorage save with `fetch('/api/order', { method: 'POST', body: JSON.stringify(formData) })`.

## 6. Legal, tax & food safety in Sweden

> **All tax rates, thresholds, and percentages below must be verified on skatteverket.se before relying on them.** These numbers change yearly. What's correct today may not be tomorrow.

### 6.1 Business registration sequence

| Step | What | Where | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Register enskild firma | verksamt.se → Skatteverket | SEK 0 |
| 2 | Apply for F-skatt (preliminary tax) | Skatteverket form SKV 4620 | SEK 0 |
| 3 | (Optional) Protect business name | bolagsverket.se | SEK 1,800 |
| 4 | Register food business | Gothenburg Miljöförvaltningen — notification, not approval | SEK 0–600 |
| 5 | VAT (moms) registration | Required when turnover exceeds ~SEK 120,000 (verify) | SEK 0 |
| 6 | Business bank account | Any Swedish bank | SEK 0–200/month |

**Food business note**: Plant-based and dairy desserts fall under simpler **registration**, not approval. You can begin operations 2 weeks after submitting the registration notification.

### 6.2 The tax structure (enskild firma)

The most important thing to understand about Swedish sole-trader tax:

**You do not have a "salary."** Your business profit IS your personal income. No payroll deductions, no employer contributions. Whatever the business profits, you pay personal tax on.

**Two tax layers, in order**:

1. **Egenavgifter** (social security contributions): ~28.97% of business profit, paid via F-skatt monthly. Egenavgifter is partly deductible against income tax, so they don't simply stack.
2. **Income tax**: ~32% (varies by Gothenburg municipality — verify at skatteverket.se), applied to profit *after* egenavgifter deduction.

**Effective combined rate on profit**: roughly 54%. But this is on *profit*, not revenue. Expenses reduce the profit base, so the effective rate on revenue is typically around 18%.

**Worked example** (approximate, illustration only):

| Line | Amount (SEK) |
|---|---|
| Revenue | 300,000 |
| Cost of goods (~30%) | -90,000 |
| Operating costs (12 months) | -90,000 |
| **Net profit** | **120,000** |
| Egenavgifter (28.97% of profit, partly deductible) | ~-25,000 |
| Income tax (~32% of remainder) | ~-30,000 |
| **Take-home** | **~SEK 65,000** |

That's ~SEK 5,400/month in your pocket from SEK 300k revenue. Normal for year one. Improves significantly as revenue scales and fixed costs stay flat.

### 6.3 VAT (moms) on food

- **Reduced rate**: 12% on food (verify on skatteverket.se)
- **Registration threshold**: ~SEK 120,000 annual turnover (verify — Sweden has changed this in recent years)
- **When required**: Once turnover exceeds the threshold, register with Skatteverket. After registration, consumer-facing prices must be displayed inclusive of VAT.
- **B2B invoices**: VAT shown separately is standard. Companies reclaim it.

### 6.4 F-skatt — preliminary tax

You pay tax monthly based on an estimated annual profit. Reconciled at year end via NE-bilaga.

**Launch strategy**: Set the F-skatt estimate **low** initially. Adjust upward as profits grow. Overpaying early ties up cash you need for operations. Skatteverket doesn't penalize modest under-estimates as long as you reconcile honestly at year end.

### 6.5 Year 1 losses can offset other income

If you operate at a loss in year one (likely given startup costs), the loss can offset other personal income from employment or other sources. You typically get ~30% of the loss back as a tax refund. This is a practical advantage of enskild firma over AB in the first year.

### 6.6 Food safety & HACCP

EU Regulation 852/2004 requires a food safety management system based on HACCP. For a small home dessert business:

- **Written cleaning and sanitation schedule** — dated, kept on file
- **Temperature log** — fridge ≤4°C, freezer ≤-18°C, checked and recorded daily
- **Allergen documentation** for every recipe
- **Batch dating and traceability** — every batch tagged with date and supplier lot
- **Food hygiene course** — Livsmedelsverket has guidance; complete a recognized Swedish-language course
- **For tiramisu specifically**: Document the egg-yolk safety control (pasteurized or cooked to 71°C). This is the single most important HACCP item for raw-egg desserts.

### 6.7 Allergen labelling

EU Regulation 1169/2011 requires declaration of 14 major allergens.

- Every retail-packaged item carries a full ingredient list and allergen declaration on its label
- Corporate deliveries include a printed allergen sheet (one per batch, not per portion)
- Cross-contamination disclosed: *"Produced in a kitchen that handles nuts, milk, eggs, gluten."*

### 6.8 Insurance

| Type | Why | Estimated cost |
|---|---|---|
| Ansvarsförsäkring (liability) | Protects against claims from allergic reactions or food safety issues | SEK 200–400/month |
| Produktansvarsförsäkring (product liability) | Recommended once you scale; often bundled | SEK 100–200/month additional |

Quote from Länsförsäkringar, If Försäkringar, or Folksam. Specify "food business, home-based, < SEK 500k turnover."

### 6.9 The körjournal — only relevant if you add a car

Required by Skatteverket to deduct car-related expenses. Log every business trip: date, start, destination, purpose, kilometres. Use Drivr or Mileage Book.

**Mileage rates (verify on skatteverket.se)**:
- Petrol/diesel: SEK 25/km
- Electric/hybrid: SEK 9.50/km

You don't have a car yet — see section 7.4. Don't worry about körjournal until you do.

## 7. Operations & supply chain

### 7.1 Kitchen requirements (home-based)

Practical requirements for a registered home food business in Sweden:

- Cleanable, non-porous surfaces (laminate, stainless, sealed wood)
- Separate prep zone for food, not shared with personal cooking during production hours
- Refrigeration with documented temperatures
- Pest-free, dust-free
- Hand-washing sink
- Documented cleaning before/after every production session
- Ingredient storage separate from personal household items

You do **not** need a commercial kitchen, certified ovens, or a separate building. Your home kitchen, used well and documented, is legal and standard for hundreds of registered small food businesses in Sweden.

### 8.3 Break-even math (tiramisu-only Phase 1)

To cover SEK 7,000/month operating costs:

- COGS per jar: ~SEK 28
- Retail price: SEK 85
- Gross margin: SEK 57 per jar
- **Jars/month to break even**: 7,000 ÷ 57 = **123 jars**

123 jars = ~30 jars/week. Realistic path:
- 2 corporate clients × one 12-jar box weekly = 96 jars/month
- + 7 individual jars/week from pickup = 28 jars/month
- **Total: 124 jars/month → break-even**

2–3 corporate clients are achievable in months 2–3 with focused outreach. Break-even by month 3–4 is plausible. Month 1–2 runs at a loss while you build the customer base.

## 12. Risk register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Food safety incident (raw-egg salmonella) | Low with pasteurized yolks | Catastrophic | Use pasteurized egg yolks from day one. Document every batch. Carry liability insurance. |
| Slow corporate client acquisition | Medium | Medium | First-month 20% discount. Free trial deliveries to 5 targets. Be persistent — B2B cycles are 4–8 weeks. |
| Spoilage from 72-hour shelf life | Medium | Medium | Made-to-order production. No stocked finished jars >24 hours unsold. Documented cold chain. |
| Capacity ceiling hit too soon | Medium | Medium-high | Build production rhythm. Book a shared commercial kitchen before max capacity. Part-time help when monthly revenue > SEK 40,000. |
| Summer (June–August) revenue dip | Medium | Medium | Lighter summer variant (berry tiramisu, panna cotta). Target Midsommar, summer parties, restaurant terrasse demand. |
| Restaurants squeezing margins | Medium | Medium | Hold firm on minimum order (24 jars). Don't accept exclusivity. Cancel accounts with margin under 30%. |
| Instagram algorithm dependency | Low–Medium | Medium | Email list from day one. Cross-post LinkedIn. Don't rely on one algorithm. |
| Year-end tax surprise | Low with monthly F-skatt | Medium | Set F-skatt low but reconcile quarterly. Use Bokio. Get a 1-hour redovisningsbyrå consultation before first year-end. |
| Solo founder burnout | Medium–high | High | Build a real rest day. Don't take orders requiring Saturday production unless margin justifies. Plan one full week off after month 6. |

## 15. Glossary of Swedish business terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Enskild firma** | Sole trader / sole proprietorship. The legal form for launch. Owner is personally liable; business profit IS owner's income. |
| **Aktiebolag (AB)** | Limited liability company. Migrate to this when annual profit exceeds ~SEK 400,000–500,000. |
| **F-skatt** | "F-tax" — preliminary tax registration that lets you invoice clients professionally. Required for B2B. |
| **Egenavgifter** | Social security contributions paid by self-employed people. ~28.97% of profit. Partly deductible. |
| **Moms** | VAT. 12% reduced rate for food. Registration required above ~SEK 120,000 turnover. |
| **NE-bilaga** | The annual tax declaration appendix that enskild firma owners file. Calculates business profit and resulting personal tax. |
| **Verksamt.se** | The government one-stop portal for business registration. |
| **Bolagsverket** | The Swedish Companies Registration Office. Protects business names. |
| **PRV** | Patent- och registreringsverket. The Swedish trademark and patent office. |
| **Skatteverket** | The Swedish Tax Agency. Where you register for F-skatt, VAT, and file annual taxes. |
| **Miljöförvaltningen** | The municipality's environmental administration. Where home food businesses register. |
| **Livsmedelsverket** | The Swedish Food Agency. Sets food safety regulations. |
| **Eget uttag** | "Personal withdrawal" — when you transfer money from the business account to your personal account. NOT taxed at point of withdrawal; profit is taxed via NE-bilaga regardless of whether withdrawn. |
| **Körjournal** | Mileage log. Required by Skatteverket for any car expenses to be deductible. |
| **Milersättning** | Mileage allowance — flat SEK 25/km (petrol) or 9.50/km (electric) deductible when using a personal car for business. |
| **Ansvarsförsäkring** | Liability insurance. |
| **Bokio / Fortnox** | Swedish cloud accounting software. Bokio has a generous free tier. |
| **Redovisningsbyrå** | Accounting firm. Worth a 1-hour consultation before your first NE-bilaga filing. |
| **Bilabonnemang** | Car subscription — month-to-month, insurance included. Most flexible option if you need a car. |
| **Delade kök** | Shared commercial kitchen. Rent time when home production capacity is maxed. |
| **Hembakat** | "Home-baked." Common Swedish term — perfectly fine for your branding. |
| **Hantverk / hantverket** | Craft / craftsmanship. The brand uses "Hantverket" as the name of TheCraft section. |
| **Fika** | The Swedish coffee-and-pastry ritual. The cultural foundation your corporate subscription business sits on. |
| **Julbord** | Christmas table / Christmas buffet. Major Q4 revenue opportunity. |
| **Vaxtastöd** | Government growth support — reduced employer contributions for the first two employees a sole trader hires. |
| **Bolagsskatt** | Corporate tax — applies to AB, not enskild firma. ~20.6% (verify). |
